# Repertoire

Original piano works only: no pop adaptations. Where a piece exists in a simplified version,
prefer the **composer's original** or an official "easy originals" book over a third-party
arrangement.

Grades are **approximate** (ABRSM/RCM-style). Many neoclassical pieces aren't officially graded,
so these come from publisher ratings and comparable exam pieces. Check a few bars of the score before
committing, and adjust this list as you go. Columns: **Stage** → see [02-syllabus.md](02-syllabus.md).

## How to use this list

- At any time, have **one stretch piece** (stage + 1), **one main piece** (current stage) and **one easy piece**
  (stage − 1, for fluency and enjoyment) on the go.
- When a piece is finished, add it to your "kept up" list and replay it weekly.
- Track pieces in your app as MusicXML with a level tag, as described in [06-app-notes.md](06-app-notes.md).
