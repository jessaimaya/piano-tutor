# Practice Routine

## The daily session (25 min)

| Min | Block | What | Track |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3 | **Warm-up / technique** | Five-finger patterns → scales/chords/arpeggios for your stage. Slow, relaxed, even | K |
| 5 | **Sight-reading** | 2–4 *new* short pieces at **one level below** your method. Play each **once**, keep going, count out loud. (Mikrokosmos, Beyer, Paul Harris, Sight Reading Factory, or your app) | R, T |
| 12 | **Main work** | The method lesson or main piece: today's 4–8 bars only. Hands separate → HT slowly → metronome up a notch | C, M |
| 5 | **Music** | Play something you already know, for enjoyment, all the way through. Or the stretch piece from your genre | M |

On days you only have 10 minutes: sight-reading + main work. Skipping the warm-up is OK; skipping reading isn't.

## How to learn a new piece

1. **Listen** to it (recording) while following the score once.
2. **Map it**: find sections, repeats, key, time signature, the hardest 2 bars.
3. **Hardest bars first.** Work in chunks of 1–4 bars; overlap the chunks by one note.
4. **Hands separate until each hand is secure** (usually 1–3 days), then HT at half tempo.
5. **Metronome ladder**: start where you make ≤ 1 error, raise 4–8 bpm after 3 clean passes.
6. **Add expression last**: dynamics, pedal, rubato, once the notes and rhythm are secure.
7. **Done** = 3 clean run-throughs in a row at target tempo, plus a recording.

## Weekly rhythm

| Day | Variation |
|---|---|
| Mon–Fri | Standard session |
| Sat | Longer if you like: replay all "kept up" pieces |
| Sun | **Record** your main piece (phone or MIDI). Listen back the next day; write one sentence in the log |

## Monthly check

- Re-take a sight-reading test (your app, or a Paul Harris / ABRSM specimen test one grade up), and note the level.
- Check the exit criteria of your current stage in [02-syllabus.md](02-syllabus.md).
- Update the repertoire: finished → "kept up", choose the next main/stretch piece.

## Practice log (template)

```
## 2026-10-14
- Technique: C/G five-finger HT, ♩=72
- Reading: Mikrokosmos 12–14 (14 rough at bar 5)
- Main: Faber U4 "Ode to Joy" HT bars 1–8 ♩=60
- Music: Fiddle Tune (Milnes)
- Note: LH thumb collapsing — keep it curved
```

The log is what your app should eventually produce automatically (see [06-app-notes.md](06-app-notes.md)).
