# Videos

Every link was checked on 2026-10-13. Since there's no teacher in this plan, the **posture and hand
videos are the closest substitute**: watch them in week 1, film yourself from the side, and compare.
Re-watch them at the start of each stage.

**Self-check from your video:** forearm level with the keys or slightly above · wrists not dropped below the keys ·
knuckles arched and fingertips curved · thumb resting on its side, not collapsed · shoulders loose · feet flat, right heel on the floor by the pedal.

## Channels worth following

- **Pianist Magazine**: short lessons by Graham Fitch and Tim Stein; technique, pedal, practice.
- **Piano Lessons On The Web** (Bill Hilton): clear beginner technique and reading.
- **Piano with Rebecca Bogart**: how to practice, written with adult learners in mind.
- **tonebase Piano**: for later; masterclasses with concert pianists.
- Avoid channels built on falling-note (Synthesia) tutorials: they skip reading, which is the skill this plan is about.
